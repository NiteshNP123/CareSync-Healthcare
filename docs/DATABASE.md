## 1. Architectural Overview

CareSync's database architecture is designed around the core principle: **One Patient, One Continuous Healthcare Journey, with Multiple Authorized Healthcare Participants.**

Instead of storing disconnected medical records or unstructured JSON blobs, CareSync utilizes a normalized, relational PostgreSQL model with strong foreign keys, cascading rules, and relational integrity across 21 core entities.

## 2. Core Entities & Schema Matrix

### 2.1 Identity & Governance Layer
1. **`users`**: Central authentication entity holding email, password hash, role (`PATIENT`, `DOCTOR`, `LAB_STAFF`, `PHARMACY_STAFF`, `CAREGIVER`, `ADMIN`), full name, and avatar.
2. **`patients`**: Extended profile linked to user, internal CareSync ID (e.g. `CS-2048-7392`), DOB, blood group, emergency contacts, verified ID status, and chronic conditions.
3. **`doctors`**: Provider profile containing medical license number, specialization, qualifications, experience years, organization affiliation, consultation fee, rating, and credential verification status (`PENDING`, `UNDER_REVIEW`, `VERIFIED`, `REJECTED`, `SUSPENDED`).
4. **`organizations`**: Entity representing Diagnostic Laboratories, Pharmacies, Clinics, and Hospitals with license registrations and branch networks.
5. **`organization_members`**: Join table mapping healthcare staff (`TECHNICIAN`, `PHARMACIST`, `ADMIN`) to their organization.
6. **`caregivers`**: Patient-authorized family members or legal guardians with granular access permissions (`APPOINTMENTS`, `TASKS`, `FOLLOW_UPS`, `MEDICATIONS`, `JOURNEY_PROGRESS`, `NOTIFICATIONS`).
7. **`doctor_availability`**: Weekly scheduling slots, consultation mode (Online/In-Clinic), and break periods.

### 2.2 Clinical & Encounter Layer
8. **`appointments`**: Bookings for both `PATIENT_TO_DOCTOR` and `DOCTOR_TO_DOCTOR` consultations, with status state machine (`REQUESTED`, `CONFIRMED`, `IN_CONSULTATION`, `COMPLETED`, `CANCELLED`).
9. **`consultations`**: Clinical encounter notes documenting symptoms, objective observations, diagnosis/assessment, treatment plan, and follow-up milestones.
10. **`prescriptions`**: Master prescription record generated by doctors.
11. **`prescription_items`**: Individual medications with dosage, frequency (e.g. "Twice daily with meals"), duration, and instructions.
12. **`investigations`**: Diagnostic test requisitions ordered by physicians (`ORDERED`, `SCHEDULED`, `SAMPLE_COLLECTED`, `PROCESSING`, `COMPLETED`, `CANCELLED`).
13. **`lab_reports`**: Published diagnostic findings with structured parameter values, biological reference ranges, abnormal flags (`NORMAL`, `HIGH`, `LOW`, `CRITICAL`), and technician verification signature.
14. **`medical_documents`**: Secure vault for clinical PDFs and imaging records.
15. **`referrals`**: Formal cross-specialty clinical handoffs between physicians.
16. **`follow_ups`**: Scheduled clinical checkpoints to review recovery or test outcomes.

### 2.3 Consent, Fulfillment & Billing Layer
17. **`access_requests`**: Doctor-initiated patient access requests with mock OTP verification and custom purpose statement.
18. **`consent_records`**: Active or revoked patient consent granting specific scopes (`CONSULTATIONS`, `PRESCRIPTIONS`, `LAB_REPORTS`, `JOURNEY`, `VITALS`, `ORDERS`).
19. **`pharmacy_orders`**: Connected dispensing lifecycle tracking itemized prescriptions from quote creation to preparation, dispatch, and delivery.
20. **`pharmacy_order_items`**: Line-item medicines in a pharmacy order with stock availability check and pricing.
21. **`payments` & `invoices`**: Sandbox payment records with transaction references, tax breakdowns, and printable PDF-ready invoice metadata.

### 2.4 The Living Timeline & Analytics Layer
22. **`healthcare_journey_events`**: The central hero entity synthesizing all clinical handoffs into a continuous chronological narrative. Each event references its originating entity (`consultation`, `lab_report`, `prescription`, `appointment`, `pharmacy_order`, `referral`).
23. **`patient_vitals`**: Longitudinal vital sign metrics (Systolic/Diastolic BP, Fasting Glucose, HbA1c, Heart Rate, Weight) powering the Health Analytics visualization suite.
24. **`notifications`**: User alert dispatch for access requests, published reports, and upcoming appointments.
25. **`audit_logs`**: Immutable, tamper-evident security audit log recording every data access, consent decision, report release, and administrative verification.

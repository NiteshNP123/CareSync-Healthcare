---
name: CareSync prototype positioning
description: Durable product-safety constraints for the CareSync healthcare coordination prototype.
---

CareSync is an independent healthcare software prototype and must not imply affiliation with or endorsement by Government of India, ABDM, ABHA, eSanjeevani, Aadhaar, or any government authority. Identity verification, AI assistance, OTP, payments, and future healthcare integrations must be clearly labeled as demo/prototype or planned unless a real authorized service is connected.

**Why:** Healthcare trust and regulatory boundaries are central to the product brief; misleading official or clinical claims would change the product's meaning and risk user harm.

**How to apply:** Preserve the independent disclaimer on public-facing surfaces and keep assistive AI outputs non-diagnostic with the verification warning.
